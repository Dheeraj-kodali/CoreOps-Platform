# 🛕 Reception Staff Quick User Guide (5-Minute Training)

Designed for fast, simple reception operation at the temple entrance.

---

## 1. Checking In New Visitors ("VISITOR ENTERED")
1. Open the **New Check-In** tab.
2. Fill in:
   - **Visitor Name**
   - **Mobile Number** (10 digits)
   - **Village / City**
   - **Purpose of Visit**
   - **Number of Members** (Use `+` / `-` buttons)
3. Tap the large gold **VISITOR ENTERED** button.
4. Message *"Visitor Entered & Registered Successfully"* appears, and welcome message is sent via WhatsApp.

---

## 2. Checking Out Visitors ("VISITOR LEFT")
1. Tap **Today's Visitors** tab.
2. Find visitor by scrolling or typing name/phone in the top search bar.
3. Tap orange **VISITOR LEFT** button on card.
4. Confirm exit in pop-up dialog. Status changes to `LEFT`, duration is computed, and thank-you message is sent.
